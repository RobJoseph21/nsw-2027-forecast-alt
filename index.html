Methodology
1.Smooth recent NSW polls (LOESS through Wikipedia's poll table) to estimate today's vote share per party.
2.Use 2023 NSW primary votes as each seat's baseline; 2024–2025 by-elections override (Pittwater, Epping, Hornsby, Northern Tablelands, Port Macquarie, Kiama).
3.For ALP, Coalition and Greens, apply uniform swing per seat: every seat shifts by the statewide swing.
4.Pool IND and OTH; apply the bloc swing to OTH first, reducing IND only if OTH is exhausted. Protects sitting Independents.
5.For One Nation, predict each seat from its Federal Senate 2025 ON share and Uni-degree share via a regression fit on SA state seats (R² = 0.87). Rescale to the polled ON target.
6.Redistribute the gap between the ON prediction and uniform swing across other parties via federal cross-flow weights (≈55% from Coalition, 19% from Labor, 17% from Other).
7.Add per-seat sophomore (+1.5pts) and retirement (-2pts) effects to the incumbent's primary; offset proportionally across other parties; re-IPF to re-pin state means.
8.Two-candidate-preferred: top three by primary, use 2023 ballot data (4.7M ballots) to eliminate the third, then apply seat-specific preference flows for winner and margin.
